Our Story

From cafeteria vendor to dining operating system.

Foodify started with a simple observation: workplace cafeterias were stuck in the past. Manual processes, zero data, and untapped culinary potential. We set out to change that.

Today, we combine world-class managed services with a SaaS platform that powers contactless ordering, subsidy management, vendor settlements, and predictive analytics for companies, campuses, and healthcare institutions.

Every engagement starts the same way — understanding your people, your space, and your culture. Then we build, run, and digitize a dining program your team will genuinely love.
